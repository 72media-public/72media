Frequently Asked Questions

Do we host videos on the server?
No, we only hold the videos up to 5 hours in cache so users can download them. After that the files are automatically deleted.
Is it safe to download from here?
Yes. Behind the scenes we use yt-dlp (using yt-dlp is a legal grey area; the software itself is a legal, neutral, open-source tool) to download video or audio. Downloading media explicitly released into the public domain or under a Creative Commons license that allows redistribution is legal.
How long are my files kept?
Completed downloads stay available for 5 hours. After that they are automatically removed from the server. You can leave the page and come back later to grab them within that window.
Can I download entire playlists?
Yes. When a playlist link is detected you will be asked whether you want the entire playlist (up to 50 videos) or only the current video. Progress for each video is shown in the history panel.
Do I need an account or any software?
No. Everything works directly in your browser. No registration, no installs, and the service is free. You can support the project via the Ko-fi link if you like.
